# Code of conduct

We want participation in `eino-viewer` to be safe, respectful, and
productive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Accept correction gracefully and focus on the impact of words and actions.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publication of private or
confidential information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports
should be sent privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
reporting channel. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive
details.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as possible, limit
disclosure to people needed for resolution, and avoid retaliation against
good-faith reporters. This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone
is publicly representing the project.
